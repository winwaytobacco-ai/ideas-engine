🟢 RISK_ON_TRENDING — green light, full speed. The market's big trend is UP (SPY above its 200-day average, averages aligned and rising) and at least 2 of the 3 voting macro dials (credit stress, VIX, financial conditions) are calm. Full idea list — breakouts and pullbacks both.

🟡 RISK_ON_RANGING — green light, but the road is bumpy. Macro is calm, but SPY is stuck moving sideways in a band instead of trending. Ideas still flow, but pullback-to-value setups are preferred — breakouts tend to fail inside a range.

🟠 TRANSITION — yellow light, signals disagree. Some dials say go, others say caution. The idea list is HALVED and sizes come down.

🔴 RISK_OFF — red light, no new buying. SPY below its 200-day average, OR credit AND volatility both stressed at once. Zero new ideas by design — the run still completes and this page still publishes, so an idle day is a report, not an outage.

Dials marked INFO (yield curve, range check) are observed but do not vote — only credit, VIX and NFCI vote, plus the SPY trend test. An INFO row is never a failure. Full decision tree: README.md.
